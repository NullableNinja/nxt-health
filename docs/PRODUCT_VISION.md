## Kintsugi product principle

N//XT is built around the belief that something can be imperfect, marked by experience, and still
become more beautiful and more whole. The product should help people accept who they are while
supporting who they want to become. Every insight must therefore be evidence-aware, non-diagnostic,
and actionable without becoming a verdict. Missing data is “not known yet,” a difficult day is
context rather than failure, and goals are invitations toward care rather than proof of worth.
