# AI architecture

AI is intentionally absent from the Phase 1 write path. The domain must remain useful without an AI
provider.

Future proposals—natural-language food entries, photo recognition, pattern explanations, and weekly
analysis—must be represented as uncertain suggestions, require user confirmation before persistence,
and preserve source, methodology, date range, and limitations. Pattern language must not become
diagnosis or causal explanation.

Private journals, wellbeing entries, medication information, and life events are sensitive by
default. AI access to those categories requires a dedicated, revocable `ai-sensitive-data` consent;
general AI enablement is not enough. The UI must show what data would leave the device, where it goes,
and how to delete or export related records.
